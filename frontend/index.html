The process

From sighting to escalation, in four verified steps

01

Verify with Aadhaar

A one-time, format-checked Aadhaar verification confirms you're a real citizen — no account is ever tied to your name, only a secure hash.

02

Report with evidence

Attach a photo, audio clip or video, pin the location, and describe what you saw. Reports are timestamped and immutable once filed.

03

Community votes

Nearby verified citizens see the report and vote it up. One Aadhaar can only vote once per report — no bots, no duplicate outrage.

04

Escalate to judiciary

Reports that cross the community threshold are automatically flagged and forwarded for judicial and administrative attention.
